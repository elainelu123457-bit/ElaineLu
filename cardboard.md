# Thinking Outside the Box
For this activity, I used cardboard to create prototypes that solve small, everyday problems. I thought about problems that my friends and I actually experience, then built simple models to show how each solution could work.

## 1. Laptop Stand
![Laptop Stand](/images/laptop-stand.jpg)
My friend sometimes gets neck pain from looking down at her laptop for a long time. I made a cardboard laptop stand that raises her laptop so she can look more straight ahead while working.

## 2. Bedside Phone Holder
![Bedside Phone Holder](/images/phone-holder.jpg)
I wanted an easier place to keep my phone next to my bed without leaving it somewhere it could fall or be hard to reach. I made a cardboard phone holder that keeps my phone in an easy-to-reach spot next to my bed.

## 3. Study Helmet
![Study Helmet](/images/helmet.jpg)
My friend gets distracted by scrolling on her phone while she is supposed to be studying. I made a cardboard study helmet that blocks her view of her phone while still leaving the front open so she can see her schoolwork.

## 4. Phone Jail
![Phone Jail](/images/phone-jail.jpg)
My other friend often gets up late because she snoozes her alarms and goes back to sleep. I made a cardboard phone jail that covers her phone so she has to get up and lift the box to reach it, while an opening still lets the alarm sound come through.
